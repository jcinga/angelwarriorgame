About the game

Short description

Angel Warrior is a painterly, one-tap flying game. Descend six realms of the abyss as the archangel, cut down demons, rescue lost souls, and face the Final Demon.

Long description

Fly down through six realms of the abyss: fire, ash, brimstone, the hive, the drowned swamp and the frozen pit. Every tap lifts your wings, and every pass of your blade can cut a demon from the sky. Rescue the kneeling souls to restore your strength, slip through columns of stone, rust, honey, rotten wood and ice, and reach the Final Demon, a beast made of everything you've fought. Defeat it and the captive souls rise free. Angel Warrior was designed by one independent developer and built with AI tools, which were used for the code, the art and the music.

Features

  • Simple one-tap controls that take practice to master
  • Six painterly realms, each with its own creatures and obstacles
  • Sword combat and soul rescues, with a strength bar instead of instant death from demons
  • A full boss fight against the Final Demon
  • A verse of Scripture after every victory
  • Orchestral soundtrack created using AI tools
  • Normal and Fast modes
  • No account, no ads and no data collection in version 1.0
