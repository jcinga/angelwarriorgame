Angel Warrior

Church Partner Agreement

Between Jerry Ching, developer of Angel Warrior ("Developer"), and ("Church").

1. Purpose

The Church may share Angel Warrior with its members. In return, the Developer pays the Church a share of a Partner Fund made up of most of what the game earns from ads, as set out below.

2. Church link and Active Players

  1. Before the Church joins, the Developer confirms it is a real, active church (for example by its address, website or a pastor's contact). The Developer then gives the Church a unique App Store link and QR code.
  2. Nothing in the game identifies the Church or the player. Apple's App Store analytics report to the Developer, as totals only, how many devices installed Angel Warrior through the Church's link and how many of them were active. The Church does not receive any information about individual players. Versions of Angel Warrior without ads, including the launch version (v1.0), collect no personal data. Versions with ads use an ad network that may collect device and usage data, as described in the Angel Warrior privacy policy, which will be updated before any version with ads is released.
  3. "Reported Active Players" for a Quarter means the number of active devices that Apple's analytics attribute to the Church's link for that Quarter. Apple attributes an install to the link only if it occurs within 24 hours of the link being opened, and does not attribute players who already had the game. Apple counts only players who turned on "Share with App Developers" in their device's analytics settings, typically a minority.
  4. "Active Players" for a Quarter means Reported Active Players multiplied by three, the Developer's estimate of the real number of players behind Apple's count. The same multiplier applies to every partner church. If Apple's share of reporting players changes materially, the Developer may change the multiplier with 60 days' written notice, applying only to later Quarters.
  5. If Apple changes or discontinues this reporting, the Developer will adopt the closest available measure and notify the Church.

3. The Partner Fund

  1. "Net Revenue" means the money the Developer actually receives from ad networks for Angel Warrior, from all players, after the networks' own fees and after any deductions, refunds or adjustments they make.
  2. A "Quarter" is a calendar quarter (January–March, April–June, July–September, October–December). For each Quarter, the Partner Fund is 75% of the Net Revenue the Developer actually receives into its bank account from ad networks during that Quarter, whenever the underlying ads were shown.
  3. A "Qualifying Church" is a partner church with at least 100 Active Players in a Quarter, as defined in section 2.
  4. The Partner Fund is divided equally among all Qualifying Churches for the Quarter.
  5. The Church's share is never based on ads viewed or clicked by its members, and is never calculated from any ad metric.
  6. The Partner Fund begins with the first version of Angel Warrior that shows ads. Earlier versions, including the launch version (v1.0), contain no ads and earn no revenue.

4. Payments

  1. The Developer pays each Qualifying Church its share of the Partner Fund for a Quarter within 60 days after that Quarter ends, by check or bank transfer.
  2. The minimum payment is $25. A smaller balance rolls over to the next Quarter and is never lost.
  3. Payments are made only from Net Revenue the Developer has actually received. Money that an ad network withholds, delays or never pays is not part of any Partner Fund until (and unless) it is received.
  4. Each payment includes a statement showing the size of the Partner Fund, the number of Qualifying Churches sharing it, the Church's Reported Active Players and Active Players, and the dollar amount paid. Statements never include ad network reports or metrics, which the networks treat as confidential.
  5. If an ad network later takes back or offsets revenue that was already included in a paid Partner Fund (for example, for invalid activity), the Developer may reduce the Church's future payments by the Church's share of that amount only: the amount taken back × 75% ÷ the number of Qualifying Churches in the Quarter it was included in. This is done only by offset against future payments. The Church never has to repay money out of pocket, unless (a) the Church received a payment by mistake that it was not owed, or (b) the reduction resulted from the Church's own breach of section 6. In those cases the Developer may ask the Church to repay that amount within 60 days.
  6. Before the first payment, the Church gives the Developer a completed IRS Form W-9 (or, for a church outside the United States, the applicable IRS Form W-8), and updates it if anything changes. The Developer may hold payments until it receives a valid form, and may withhold or report amounts as the law requires.

5. Founding partner rate and changes

  1. The 75% rate and the equal split remain fixed for 12 months from the date the first version with ads is released (the "Founding Partner Rate"), unless an ad network, Apple or the law requires a change (section 10). Either party may still end this agreement under section 10.
  2. After that, the Developer may change the rate or the qualifying number with at least 60 days' written notice. Any change applies only to revenue earned after the change takes effect, never to revenue already earned.

6. Promoting the game

  1. The Church may promote Angel Warrior freely, using its own link: announcements, bulletins, slides and social posts.
  2. The Church will not: (a) ask or encourage anyone to view or click ads; (b) offer or give anyone money, prizes, credit, recognition or anything else of value for installing Angel Warrior, opening the Church's link, playing, viewing or clicking ads, or rating or reviewing the game; (c) ask anyone for a particular rating or review, or post or arrange fake ratings or reviews; (d) tell members that playing or watching ads raises money; or (e) use extra devices, emulators, scripts, paid players or any other artificial means to generate installs, plays or ad views. Ad networks and Apple prohibit these practices, and they can close the Developer's ad account, which would end payments to every partner church.
  3. If the Church breaks this section, the Developer may end this agreement immediately, withhold any revenue connected to the prohibited activity, and remove the Church from the Partner Fund.

7. Age and privacy

Angel Warrior is rated 13+ on the App Store. The Church will not direct its promotion at children under 13. Player privacy is handled under the Angel Warrior privacy policy.

8. No guaranteed earnings

The Partner Fund depends on how many people play Angel Warrior and on ad rates, which change. The Developer does not guarantee any amount of revenue. Any estimate the Developer gives of players, revenue or payments is only an estimate.

9. Names and logos

The Developer may list the Church as a partner by name. The Church may use the Angel Warrior name, logo and screenshots, as provided by the Developer and without alteration, only to promote the game. The Developer may ask the Church to stop using them at any time.

10. Pausing or ending the program

  1. Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice (email is enough). The Church's share for completed Quarters is paid with the next quarterly payment, even if it is under $25.
  2. The Developer may pause or end the partner program immediately if an ad network, Apple or the law requires it, or if the Developer's ad account is suspended. Amounts already received for completed Quarters are still paid.

11. General

  • The parties are independent. This agreement does not create a partnership, employment or agency relationship.
  • The Church tells the Developer its tax status, for example whether it is a church or religious organization treated as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, and will tell the Developer promptly if that changes. Payments are a share of revenue in return for promotion, not a donation or charitable contribution by the Developer, and the Church will not issue the Developer a donation receipt for them. Each party is responsible for its own taxes, filings and records, including any tax on the Church's income from this program. The Developer makes no statement about how payments are taxed for the Church.
  • To the extent the law allows: (a) neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, even if told they were possible; and (b) each party's total liability to the other under or relating to this agreement is limited to the greater of (i) the total amounts the Developer paid to the Church under this agreement in the 12 months before the claim arose, or (ii) $100. These limits do not apply to (1) the Developer's obligation to pay Partner Fund amounts actually owed under sections 3 and 4, (2) either party's obligations under section 12, or (3) fraud or intentional misconduct.
  • Angel Warrior and the partner program are provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, to the extent the law allows.
  • The person signing for the Church confirms that they are authorized to sign this agreement on the Church's behalf and to bind the Church, and that the Church's legal name, address and (if any) EIN given below are correct.
  • This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. If a dispute arises, the parties will first try in good faith to resolve it by email and, if either asks, by a video or phone call, for at least 30 days before starting any formal proceeding. If it is not resolved, either party may bring it only in the state or federal courts nearest the Developer's home address in California. Either party may instead use small-claims court where it qualifies. Each party pays its own costs, unless a court orders otherwise.
  • This is the entire agreement. Changes must be in writing, except rate changes made under section 5.

12. Responsibility for claims

  1. Each party will defend and cover the other against third-party claims, and the resulting losses and reasonable costs (a "Covered Claim"), to the extent they arise from (a) that party's own content and materials, including in the Church's case its announcements, posts and other promotional materials; (b) that party's names, logos or trademarks as provided to the other; or (c) that party's breach of this agreement, violation of law, or intentional misconduct.
  2. For the Church, (c) includes any breach of section 6 (Promoting the game) or section 7 (Age and privacy).
  3. A party asking for coverage must tell the other promptly, let it control the defense, and cooperate reasonably. Neither party may settle a Covered Claim in a way that admits fault for the other or binds the other without its written consent.
