Angel Warrior is a free iPhone and iPad game about St. Michael's fight against the dark: the archangel descends into the abyss, cuts down demons and rescues lost souls.

How it works

  1. Your church gets its own link

    We create a link and a QR code for your church. Put it in the bulletin, on a slide, or in the youth group chat.

  2. Members install through it

    Anyone who installs Angel Warrior through your link is counted toward your church. Nobody signs up or shares personal details.

  3. Your church receives its share

    Every quarter we pay your church, with the report that shows how the amount was calculated.

How your church code works

  1. Open your church's Join page

    Members open your church's Join page from the link or QR code.

  2. Install from the App Store

    The Join page sends them to the App Store to install Angel Warrior for free.

  3. Enter the church code in the game

    They type your church code in the game. The phone stores only a church ID, nothing personal.

The code only tells us which church to credit. It never unlocks features or rewards the player.

Where things stand

Angel Warrior releases on the App Store on November 1, 2026. At launch (v1.0) the game has no ads, so the church share begins with our first update, which adds them. Churches that join now get their link ready for that update, and their members can start playing right away.

What to expect

Modest at first, and it grows with the game. This is a steady, ongoing share, not a one-time fundraiser. Mobile game ads earn a fraction of a cent each, so the amounts start small.

The partner fund is the bigger part, and it grows with every player around the world. Small churches receive the same slice as large ones. With 100 partner churches instead of 20, each church's fund share is a fifth as much: about $540 a year at 100,000 players, and $5,400 at 1,000,000.

Estimates, not promises. They assume your members are active, with about half playing on a given day and seeing a couple of ads, and that most players worldwide aren't linked to a church. More regular play, or the busy season before Christmas, earns more. Payments are made quarterly.

Every payment comes with the numbers from the ad report for your church, so you can see exactly what your members' play earned.

The terms, in plain words

  • Based on what we actually receive. Ad networks keep about a third of what advertisers pay before any money reaches us. Both shares are figured from what's left.
  • Who shares in the fund. Any partner church with at least 25 members who played during the quarter. We confirm each church is real when it joins.
  • Founding partner rates. The 100% member share and the 75% fund are guaranteed for the first 12 months after ads launch. After that they can only change with 60 days' notice, and never for money already earned.
  • Quarterly payments, $25 minimum. Smaller amounts roll over to the next quarter. Nothing is lost.
  • Real players only. Share the game freely, but please never ask members to watch or click ads, and never use extra phones or programs to play. Ad networks catch this, don't pay for it, and can shut off earnings for every church.

Questions

Does it cost the church anything?
No. The game is free to download and joining is free.
Is this a donation?
It's a revenue share: all of the ad income from your members' play, plus an equal share of the partner fund.
Why does every church get the same share of the fund?
So small churches benefit as much as large ones from the game's growth. Large churches still earn more through their own members' share.
What information do you collect about our members?
In v1.0, nothing: your church code is stored only on the phone as a church ID, and Apple reports link installs to us only as totals. No names, emails or contacts. The ad-supported update (v1.1) will add an ad network, and the privacy policy and App Store privacy label will be updated before that update is released.
Who is the game for?
It's rated 13+ on the App Store for fantasy violence (sword fighting against demons). The theme is spiritual warfare, and the win screen closes with Matthew 25:23.
When and how are payments made?
Quarterly, by check or bank transfer, within 45 days after each quarter ends, once your church's balance reaches $25.
Can the percentages change?
Both are fixed for the first 12 months after ads launch. After that, any change needs 60 days' notice and applies only going forward.
