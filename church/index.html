Angel Warrior is a free iPhone and iPad game about St. Michael's fight against the dark: the archangel descends into the abyss, cuts down demons and rescues lost souls.

How it works

  1. Share the game

    Put the game's QR code in the bulletin, on a slide, or in the group chat. We'll send it to you.

  2. Members install the game and pick your church

    The first time they open it, the game asks "Playing with a church?" They tap your church's name once, and that's it. Nobody signs up or shares personal details.

  3. Your church receives its share

    Every quarter we pay your church its share of the partner fund, with a statement that shows how the amount was calculated.

Where things stand

Angel Warrior releases on the App Store on November 1, 2026. At launch (v1.0) the game has no ads, so the church share begins with our first update, which adds them. Churches that join now are added to the game's church list for that update, and their members can start playing right away.

What to expect

Modest at first, and it grows with the game. This is a steady, ongoing share, not a one-time fundraiser. Mobile game ads earn a fraction of a cent each, so the amounts start small.

The terms, in plain words

  • Based on what we actually receive. Ad networks keep about a third of what advertisers pay before any money reaches us. The fund is all of what's left, from every player in the world, shared among qualifying partner churches (25+ active players) in proportion to their active players. We keep none of the ad network revenue. Optional purchases, sponsorships and ads we sell directly are separate and support the game.
  • Never based on ads watched. Your church's share doesn't depend on how many ads your members see. Players are never asked or rewarded to view ads.
  • Who shares in the fund. Any partner church with at least 25 active players in the quarter. "Active" means a player who picked your church in the game and played on at least six different days that quarter. The count is exact, and we'll email you your number during the quarter so there are no surprises. Churches below 25 that quarter don't share in it, and the whole fund goes to the churches that do. If no church qualifies, the fund carries over to the next quarter. We confirm each church is real when it joins.
  • Founding partner rate. The 100% fund and the player-based split stay fixed for the first 12 months after ads launch, unless an ad network, Apple or the law requires a change. After that they can only change with 60 days' notice, and never for money already earned. Either side can end the partnership with 30 days' notice.
  • Quarterly payments, $25 minimum, within 60 days of quarter end. Smaller amounts roll over to the next quarter. Nothing is lost. Statements show the size of the fund, the number of churches sharing it, your active players and the dollar amount paid.
  • Real players only. Share the game freely, but please never ask members to watch or click ads, and never use extra phones or programs to play. When you share it, say "our church is a partner," not "play to raise money for us." Ad networks catch the latter, don't pay for it, and can shut off earnings for every church.

Questions

Does it cost the church anything?
No. The game is free to download and joining is free.
What counts as an active player?
Someone who picked your church in the game and played on at least six different days during the quarter. Installing without playing doesn't count, and nobody is counted twice.
Is this a donation?
It's a revenue share: 100% of the ad network revenue the game receives (not in-app purchases, sponsorships or ads we sell directly) goes into a fund for partner churches, shared by active players, and qualifying churches receive their share quarterly once it reaches $25.
How is my church's share worked out?
Your piece of the pie depends on how many active players your church has. If it has 1 in 10 of the active players of all qualifying partner churches (25 or more) that quarter, it receives 1 in 10 of the fund. Nothing depends on counting ads.
What information do you collect about our members?
Nothing personal. Today the game sends nothing anywhere. From the first update, it remembers only which church a player picked and sends us a daily count per church. No names, emails or contacts. The ad-supported update (v1.1) will add an ad network, and the privacy policy and App Store privacy label will be updated before that update is released.
Who is the game for?
It's rated 13+ on the App Store for fantasy violence (sword fighting against demons). The theme is spiritual warfare, and every victory closes with a verse of Scripture, starting with Matthew 25:23.
When and how are payments made?
Quarterly, by check or bank transfer, within 60 days after each quarter ends, once your church's balance reaches $25.
Can the terms change?
The 100% and the player-based split stay fixed for the first 12 months after ads launch, unless an ad network, Apple or the law requires a change. After that, any change needs 60 days' notice and applies only going forward. Either side can end the partnership with 30 days' notice.
