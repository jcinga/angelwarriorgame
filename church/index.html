Angel Warrior is a free iPhone and iPad game about St. Michael's fight against the dark: the archangel descends into the abyss, cuts down demons and rescues lost souls.

How it works

  1. Your church gets its own App Store link

    We create an App Store link and QR code just for your church. Put it in the bulletin, on a slide, or in the group chat.

  2. Members scan and install

    That's it. Apple reports to us how many players came through your link and how many are active. Nobody signs up, types a code, or shares personal details.

  3. Your church receives its share

    Every quarter we pay your church its share of the partner fund, with a statement that shows how the amount was calculated.

Where things stand

Angel Warrior releases on the App Store on November 1, 2026. At launch (v1.0) the game has no ads, so the church share begins with our first update, which adds them. Churches that join now get their link ready for that update, and their members can start playing right away.

What to expect

Modest at first, and it grows with the game. This is a steady, ongoing share, not a one-time fundraiser. Mobile game ads earn a fraction of a cent each, so the amounts start small.

The terms, in plain words

  • Based on what we actually receive. Ad networks keep about a third of what advertisers pay before any money reaches us. The fund is 75% of what's left, from every player in the world, split evenly among partner churches.
  • Never based on ads watched. Your church's share doesn't depend on how many ads your members see, and we never ask anyone to watch them.
  • Who shares in the fund. Any partner church whose link shows at least 30 active players in Apple's report for the quarter. Apple only counts players who chose to share analytics with developers, about one in three, so 30 reported is roughly 100 real players. Every church is measured the same way. We confirm each church is real when it joins.
  • Founding partner rate. The 75% fund and the even split are guaranteed for the first 12 months after ads launch. After that they can only change with 60 days' notice, and never for money already earned.
  • Quarterly payments, $25 minimum. Smaller amounts roll over to the next quarter. Nothing is lost. Statements show dollar amounts, your active members and the number of churches sharing.
  • Real players only. Share the game freely, but please never ask members to watch or click ads, and never use extra phones or programs to play. Ad networks catch this, don't pay for it, and can shut off earnings for every church.

Questions

Does it cost the church anything?
No. The game is free to download and joining is free.
Is this a donation?
It's a revenue share: 75% of what the game earns from ads goes into a fund for partner churches, split evenly, and your church receives its share every quarter.
Why does every church get the same share?
So small churches benefit from the game's growth as much as large ones, and so nobody's share depends on counting ads. Every player a church brings in grows the fund for all.
What information do you collect about our members?
Nothing. Apple reports your link's installs and active players to us only as totals, and the game itself stores nothing about your church. No names, emails or contacts. The ad-supported update (v1.1) will add an ad network, and the privacy policy and App Store privacy label will be updated before that update is released.
Who is the game for?
It's rated 13+ on the App Store for fantasy violence (sword fighting against demons). The theme is spiritual warfare, and every victory closes with a verse of Scripture, starting with Matthew 25:23.
When and how are payments made?
Quarterly, by check or bank transfer, within 45 days after each quarter ends, once your church's balance reaches $25.
Can the terms change?
The 75% and the even split are fixed for the first 12 months after ads launch. After that, any change needs 60 days' notice and applies only going forward. We'd only pause the program if an ad network or Apple required it.
