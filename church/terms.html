Angel Warrior

Partner Church Program Terms

Version: October 2026 · Jerry Ching, developer of Angel Warrior

Angel Warrior is a free action game for iPhone and iPad about the archangel Michael, rated 13+, designed and built by me, Jerry Ching, as a solo developer using AI tools for the code, art and music. It launches November 1, 2026. This program is my way of thanking churches that share it.

A thank-you program

This is a voluntary program I run in good faith as described here. Nobody signs anything. Partner payments are made in return for sharing the game, but they are not guaranteed, and I don't promise any amount.

Joining

Email angelwarriorapp@gmail.com with your church's name, address, website and a contact person, and include this line:

"I've read the Partner Church Program Terms (version October 2026) and agree to follow the sharing rules."

By sending it, you confirm you can act for your church. I'll confirm it's a real, active church, add it to the game's church list, and send the game's QR code with a welcome email. Members pick your church in the game, when first asked or later under "My church."

How the partner payments work

  • The fund: each quarter, 100% of the ad network revenue I actually receive for Angel Warrior, from all players, after network fees and adjustments. In-app purchases, sponsorships and ads I sell directly aren't included.
  • Who shares: churches with at least 25 Active Players that quarter, in proportion to their Active Players. Churches below 25 don't share that quarter. If none qualifies, the fund carries over to the next quarter.
  • Active Player: someone who chose your church and played on at least six different days in the quarter. Installing without playing doesn't count. Players count for one church at a time and may switch; each counts once per quarter, for the church chosen last. One Apple Account across devices counts as one player. You can see your current count anytime. If a platform forces a counting change, I'll use the closest measure and tell you.
  • Never based on ads anyone views or clicks.
  • Start: with the first update that has ads. The launch version has none.

No guarantees

Amounts aren't guaranteed and may be zero. They depend on players and ad rates, which I don't control. Examples on our website are illustrations, not promises.

Payments and taxes

Partner payments go out quarterly by check or bank transfer, within about 60 days after the quarter ends, with a statement showing the fund size, total qualifying Active Players, your church's count and the amount. The minimum is $25, and smaller amounts roll over. If an ad network takes back money already shared, I may reduce your church's future payments by its share. I'll never ask for repayment except of money sent by mistake.

Partner payments are made only to the church itself, in its legal name, to its own bank account or published address, never to an individual. Before the first payment, and before any change to payment details, I'll confirm using the church's published contact details (such as the email or phone number on its website).

Before the first payment, please send a W-9 (or W-8 outside the U.S.) and your tax status. I may hold payments until then. Partner payments are made in return for sharing the game; they are not donations, so please don't issue a donation receipt for them. Each church handles its own taxes.

Sharing the game

Share it freely. Sample line: "Our church is an Angel Warrior partner. Share the game, share the blessings." Don't ask members to play, watch or click ads to raise money. Just share the game. Never reward installs, plays or reviews, ask for a particular rating, or use extra devices, emulators, scripts or paid players. Apple and ad networks can cut off income for every church over this, so I may remove a church that does it and hold back related payments. Please don't aim promotion at children under 13.

You may use the logo and screenshots I provide, unchanged, and add your church's name alongside them. I may list your church as a partner by name.

Privacy

I receive only daily counts per church, never names, emails or other personal details, and churches get nothing about individual players. The ad update adds an ad network that may collect device and usage data. The privacy policy will be updated before that release.

Changes, leaving and ending

  • Leaving: anytime, by email. Shares for completed quarters are still sent, even under $25.
  • Founding period: I intend to keep 100% and the player-based split for 12 months after ads launch.
  • Changes: after that, at least 60 days' email notice before changing the percentage or the 25-player threshold, going forward only. Each new version of these terms gets a new date.
  • Ending: 30 days' email notice, or immediately if Apple, an ad network or the law requires it, or my ad account is suspended. Money received for completed quarters will still be shared.
