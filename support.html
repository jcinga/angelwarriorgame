How to play

Tap to fly. Each tap lifts the angel's wings. Fly into demons with your sword out in front to cut them down, and fly low over the kneeling souls to rescue them. Touching a column ends the run. Demons and bumps drain your strength bar; rescued souls restore it.

Descend through six realms. At the bottom waits the Final Demon.

Common questions

There is no sound.
Check the SOUND ON / SOUND OFF switch in the top right of the home screen, and your phone's volume.
The game feels slow or choppy.
Turn off Low Power Mode (Settings → Battery). It limits games to half their normal frame rate.
What is Fast mode?
The same game at a quicker pace. Fast mode keeps its own best score.
Does the game collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
