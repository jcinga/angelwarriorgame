Angel Warrior

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Developer: Jerry Ching

Angel Warrior collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, the game keeps a few small values on your own device:

  • your best score in Normal and Fast mode
  • whether sound is on or off

These never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
  • It does not share or sell any information, because it has none to share.

Apple services

If you test the game through TestFlight or download it from the App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example crash reports you choose to share with developers. That data is handled by Apple, not by this game.

Children

Angel Warrior is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that changes how data is handled, such as optional ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
