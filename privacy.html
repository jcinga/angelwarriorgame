Angel Warrior

Privacy Policy

Effective with version 1.1 · Developer: Jerry Ching

Angel Warrior does not collect your name, email or any account details. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and if you pick a partner church it sends one anonymous line a day so that church's players can be counted.

What the game stores on your device

  • your best score in Normal and Fast mode, and the speed you last played
  • whether sound is on or off
  • the partner church you picked, if any
  • a random player number the game makes up the first time it runs (it is not tied to you, your Apple Account or your device)

If iCloud is turned on, the church you picked and the random player number are also kept in your own iCloud storage, so your iPhone and iPad share them. Deleting the app deletes what is on the device.

Partner church counting

If you pick a partner church, once a day when you play the game sends three things to our counting sheet: the date, the church you picked and your random player number. Nothing else is sent: no name, email, location, contacts or device identifier.

We use it only to count how many players each church has (players who played on at least six different days in a quarter) and to work out and document partner payments. Churches see only their total count, never anything about an individual player. The counting sheet is stored with Google (Google Sheets). If you do not pick a church, nothing is sent.

Advertising

Version 1.1 shows full-screen ads between some games, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device type and settings, IP address (which can suggest an approximate location), and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not give Google access to your device's advertising identifier. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent message lets you choose how your data is used for ads.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
  • It does not sell any information.

Apple services

If you test the game through TestFlight or download it from the App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example crash reports you choose to share with developers. That data is handled by Apple, not by this game.

Children

Angel Warrior is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
